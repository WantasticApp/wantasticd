Welcome to Wantastic

This installs the Wantastic secure network agent on this Mac:

  • a system service that keeps this Mac connected to your network, starting with macOS;
  • the Wantastic menu-bar app for status, peers and exit nodes.

After installing, click Log in in the menu-bar icon to add this Mac to your account in the browser. No password or code is needed here.

Installing again upgrades in place and keeps your login.
